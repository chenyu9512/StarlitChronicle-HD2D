IMPORT AND READINESS
PNG files retain their supplied resolution and alpha. Source sheets and separated sprites overlap subjects; file totals are not unique design counts. Repaired replacements are existing subjects, not extra designs. Resampled background alternatives are explicitly labelled and contain no extra painted detail. Read inventory.json for pixel dimensions, pivots, scale notes and warnings. Legacy pivots and repaired visible-bounds pivots need checking against your intended ground contact. No single pixels-per-metre scale is promised. Keep calm ground under combat and decorative art beside it; test overlay/path joins.
